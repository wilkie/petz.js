## The traces

[[measured]] Each saved trace, the switches its driver ran with (as `catzpen_test` names them; the file's name is made of them and its frames), how many frames, and the states of its own (`+0x2552`) the cat was in as a tick began. Every one replays as the reimplementation (`HARNESS_REUSE=1`, with its switches); the notes below say what each first met and held. The traces are not in the repository: `oracle/build/harness/`, made again by running the test with its switches (on winbox.js's Rust engine, its `trace` example). A switch changed since a trace was made makes another day if run again: `TRACE_LIVELY` held liveliness alone for `-POUNCE-LIVELY-4000` and added independence and seriousness for `-SPRAY-LIVELY-4000`, sickness after; `TRACE_TOY` took the yarn alone for `-POUNCE-TOY-4000`, the three toys for `-6000`, and the cheese too for `-8000` and `-POUNCE-TOY-FAR-4000`.

## The playpen harness

- [[measured]] CATZDLL.DLL's playpen runs in the harness ([[guide:reproducing]]) as CATZ.EXE runs it (CATZ.WAD seg4:12eb on), around a cat adopted on the oracle: the parameters the game filled in (`PetParams`, its `ds:13f0`), taken from CATZ.EXE running on winbox.js as `InitPetModule` begins, with the driver's own window and instance; `InitGlobalPetzData`, a `PetModule` and `InitPetModule`, `XApt::InitTree`, and then a frame at a time `OpenScreenDrawPort`, `DoDrawFrame` and `CloseScreenDrawPort` (`harness/catzpen.c`). The cat is the root apt's 0x1d (`InitPetModule`, seg34:1353). On winbox.js's virtual clock a run is the same every time, the cat's states, actions, brains and C's `rand` all.
- [[measured]] winbox.js's TypeScript engine is retired; the harness now runs on its Rust engine (its `trace` example, `target/release/examples/trace`, or `WINBOX_TRACE`): the driver's folder as `C:\CORPUS\CATZPEN` with its extras on a drive C: of its own, over winbox.js's Windows installed for 256 colours (`oracle/build/drive-c-vga256`), `--display vga256`, ending as the driver ends Windows. The 2,000 frames' day ran in 3 minutes; its 2,002 ticks are the TypeScript engine's, state for state, a brain moved on by the clock differing by 1 or 2 at two of them, and replayed every field agrees (3,023 the same). Only taking `PetParams` from CATZ.EXE (`catz-params.bin`) still needs the TypeScript probe, which watched its calls. Saved traces are read again without either (`HARNESS_REUSE`).

## A cat's day

- [[measured]] The playpen harness can follow a cat through its day (`TRACE.TXT`, 2,000 frames, no cases): the cat's machine's slot 4 is wrapped, so each tick of a state of its own -- not those its entering calls -- is written with C's `rand` seed and every field its handlers read, and then what it did, the recorders for its actions, head, steering and both machines' `NewState` passing each call on. The copy of the cat's class's table must be longer than 0x300 bytes: a slot past that is called at frame 536.
- [[measured]] Over 2,000 frames from the playpen as CATZ.EXE leaves it, the cat idles (6), grooms (13) and plays (15): 765 ticks walking on (23), 733 looking at things (12), 186 readying a walk (22), 127 in its moment (21), 105 sharpening its claws (32), and 62 and 24 in 57 and 56, swatting. In 108 ticks it acted -- pushed an action or asked for a state, its script done -- and the 79 of those held in states reimplemented, where they are (`catzStateTick`, and `catzMomentTick` for the moment; swatting from how the toy lay to the paw, as the trace has the engine give it), are as the engine's: the first state of its own asked for, or the first global state, and, where neither, the first action pushed. Walking on, whether `PopScript` found what it walks to is not written, and the reimplementation is held either way; a state the cat is put in is entered at once, and what its entering asks for -- a global state's for one of the cat's own, or the cat's own for a global state -- is not held, nor a first arrival's transition to a neutral pose. Not held: grooming by its ways 4, 5, 8 and 9, which are not reimplemented.
- [[measured]] What a handler asks after popping its script it must be given as it was then, not as the tick began: popping moves the cat and what it plays with. The trace keeps what the swat's `GetOrientationInfo` (slot `0x304`) and `GetSwatMissDistance` (`0x308`) answered within the tick -- recorders in the copy of the cat's table, as for its head -- for, as the toy rolls nearer, may-end answers by them: one day, the paw 118, 112, then 101 from the toy as three ticks began, it swatted again on the third, nearer than 100 by the time it asked. A swat cued to strike turns its head to the toy first, its only call to its head outside entering, so a swat that does is a strike: the trace keeps, for it, whether the toy is on the shelf, whether the shelf is closed and the toy's `+0x1d0`, and how the toy lay as the swat asked a second time. The trace also takes `ScriptSprite::IsCued` (966) as it takes `rand`, keeping the cues the cat's handler asks and the answers; and with `TRACE_CUES=1` it cues the cat itself on every other tick of swatting (56) and begging (35), to strike (5) and to look (6), so that the engine runs what it does then, a day of its own (`CATZTRACE-CUES.OUT`). Over 2,000 frames so cued, the cat was cued to strike in 71 ticks, and in the 15 in which it acted it swung or went to may-end, all as the reimplementation: the strike's knocking the toy and walking off were not met so -- they are over 6,000 frames on the Rust engine (`CATZTRACE-CUES-6000.OUT`): swatting to walking off (56 to 22) once, among 26 swatting ticks and 18 of may-end in which it acted, all as the reimplementation, its swat's fields with them.
- [[measured]] Which pushes are the handler's own is told by who pushed: the recorder keeps its caller's code segment, and a push from `ScriptSprite`'s code -- seg41 (`PopScript`) or seg40 (`ResetStack`) -- is the script going on, so the first push the handler made itself is held; no tick is guessed at. A tick a state is entered in is not held but for walking (22) and sharpening its claws (32), whose entering is their work. A push may draw its script at random (`ScriptRand`), and a handler pops its script, which may push the next actions, before it draws; and `PopScript` may draw again after its last push, unrecorded. So the trace takes C's `rand` itself (seg1:4b21): a far jump written over its start, through a data alias of its code (KERNEL's `AllocCSToDSAlias`, 170), sends each call to the driver, which draws as it does and keeps the seed before each draw and the code segment that called it; the handler's draws begin at the first that script code (seg40, `ScriptRand` among it, and seg41) did not make, and after the script's last push: a push of the script's may draw for itself, called from the push and not from script code -- one tick asleep, its script's 0x144 drew three so before the cat slept on, which, taken for the handler's, had it dream (0x25b). Before, keeping only the seed after each push, one tick asleep could not be placed: its script's 0x144 drew three and `PopScript` one more before the cat slept on. Entered, prestalking's pushes are held, not whether its script, popped at once, is done and it stalks.
- [[measured]] The day is the same from run to run of one driver, but a driver that writes more takes more of winbox.js's virtual clock, and past 2,000 frames or so the day goes otherwise: each change to what the trace writes has given a new day, the cat eating, swatting at a rolling toy, or asleep for 7,000 ticks.
- [[measured]] With C's `rand` taken, the day was the hunting day again (530 ticks stalking, 1,650 looking at things, 1,340 in its moment, 2,812 walking on): all 302 ticks held in states reimplemented are as the engine's, none unseeded, and one, begging, reads what is not reimplemented. In four ticks it was cued to strike, and in none of them did it act, so the strike is not yet measured.
- [[measured]] On another day, the cat slept through most of 10,000 frames (7,739 ticks asleep, 800 walking on, 733 looking at things, 240 in its moment); of 157 ticks in which it acted, 125 held in states reimplemented are as the engine's, and one, unseeded, is set aside.
- [[measured]] With the cues taken, the 10,000 frames were that sleeping day again, about four hours on winbox.js: all 126 ticks held are as the engine's, none unseeded. It never pounced, and no day yet has stalked (2).
- [[measured]] So with `TRACE_POUNCE=1` (`TRACE.TXT`'s second number 2) the driver puts the cat to pounce itself, as stalking does (`CatSprite::NewState`, 497, with 3), on every fourth tick of stalking or swatting (56, 57) at something it plays with, a day of its own (`CATZTRACE-POUNCE.OUT`); the tick it leaves and the one it enters are followed. Over 2,000 frames so put, the cat was 206 ticks pouncing and 52 stalking, and all 17 pouncing ticks in which it acted are as the reimplementation: 13 entered not readied (`+0x1500` not 0x14), stalking again; two entered leaping, under 200 away (0x20f); one done that walks to its prey (22) and one that idles (6). It was never cued to catch, and its other leaps are not yet met.
- [[measured]] Put to pounce, the driver also cues the cat to catch (2) on every other tick of pouncing. Over 4,000 frames so, all 28 pouncing ticks in which it acted are as the reimplementation, these besides: leaping farther under 200 away (0x21b), 0xdf before a leap, cued to catch and done without it, idling (6), and, having caught the mouse (`+0xee0` set), its victim (5). The cat then held its victim 1,061 ticks (5) and watched lazily 225 (30); the cursor's moment, the food it eats when cued, and the leaps farther than 200 are not yet met. With `TRACE_FAR=1` (`TRACE.TXT`'s 1073741824) too, the driver puts the cat to pounce on stalking's first tick, its prey still far, not its fourth; over 4,000 frames on the Rust engine with the toys (`CATZTRACE-POUNCE-TOY-FAR-4000.OUT`) one pounce leapt from farther than 200, not side on to it (0x35), and all 9 pouncing ticks it acted in, its entering's pushes among them, are as the reimplementation. Side on (0x2c5) it was not met; nor after the mouse over 6,000 frames (`CATZTRACE-POUNCE-FAR-6000.OUT`: two pounces, both nearer than 200, all held as the reimplementation) -- side on asks the cat to face, and its prey to lie, 33 to 95 across, and far.
- [[measured]] With its victim (5) reimplemented, that day's 8 ticks it acted in with the mouse are as the reimplementation, each 0x232, not inclined to behaviour 28; and so is the standard day. Its wrestling (6), letting go and carrying (7) were not met so (all are since, in the pounce, lively and toy traces below). One of them drew once, as its script went on, from the cat's code its script's action called -- not from script code, so taken for the handler's. So a draw is now the script's where a return address into its code (seg40, seg41) is on the stack below the tick's, as well as where it called `rand` itself (the push's own draws among them).
- [[measured]] Put to pounce, the driver also puts the cat with its victim (5) to wrestle it (6) on every fourth tick, as its handler does, and cues it (7) on every other tick with its victim, so that it makes its sounds. - [[measured]] With `TRACE_LIVELY=1` (`TRACE.TXT`'s 268435456) the driver holds the cat's liveliness at 100 -- with its victim, having done something, it would let go one time in three -- and, put to pounce, leaves its victim to it, not putting it to wrestle. - [[read out]] `MapAndPlayCatSound` (seg15:11ae) asks first how many layered frames are left (its slot `0xa8`): where any are, it neither chooses (`MapCatSound`, which draws) nor plays. - [[measured]] With `TRACE_TOY=1` (`TRACE.TXT`'s 536870912) the driver puts the yarn ball (6) off the shelf into the pen and, on every tenth tick of looking at things, passing the time, its moment, walking or grooming, puts the cat to attack it (global 1, `+0x35a4` 6); with pouncing asked (`TRACE_POUNCE`), the yarn is its victim.
