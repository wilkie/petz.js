Part of [[topic:catz-behaviour]], how a Catz cat behaves: its addresses are CATZDLL.DLL's, `seg:offset`, segments in decimal. "Above" and "below" may mean that page.

## Closed loop

- [[measured]] The bookkeeping: after a tick held in which the cat kept its state, what the reimplementation says that tick wrote -- its count (`+0x3502`), `+0x34ca`, its mode (`+0x3508`), `+0x35a6`, `+0x35ac`, `+0x35ee`, `+0x35a4` and `+0x33be` -- is held against what the engine's fields are as the next tick of the same state and global state begins, neither entered nor left (a reflex may begin another state in its `Update`, and what puts it in another may write first), and no frame between without a tick (the driver forcing one). First over 14 traces it found what the per-tick holding could not: petting counts on (`+0x3502`) every tick its script is done, whatever it does; waiting for a treat pops its script again after each push and counts on; and sprayed (63) runs on from entering into its tick, its tint counting to 1 at once and on to 9. Now all agree: 132 fields on the 10,000 frames' day, 128 petted, 150 pouncing, 98 scared, 91 with the treat, and the rest.
- [[measured]] Carried on: kept through the ticks the cat does not act in, by what each of its states writes every tick whether or not its script is done (`catzStateBetween`) -- asleep, its count on to 100 and back to 0 with an energy more; sprayed, its tint counted to 9; dreaming badly, its count 1 cued 7 and morphed on; suckling the user, nursed cued 0; looking at things, its count on with each look (a head turn, which the trace's tick does not count as acting) -- and held where it next acts, it agrees on 33,000 fields over the 14 traces: 8,659 on the 10,000 frames' day, 4,269 scared, 3,081 petted, 2,419 napping. Walking on counts its strides (seg25:0bbf) where its script is done and it walks on pushing nothing, which the trace did not show (nothing of its script left after the handler is not it: so taken, 95 more disagreed). So the driver jumps over `ScriptSprite::PopScript` (992) too -- putting its first bytes back to call it and jumping over them again, by a code alias kept for the day -- and the tick's line keeps how often the cat's script was popped and what the first pop answered (bit 1, done): over a 2,000 frames' day (`CATZTRACE.OUT`) 1,767 ticks popped once and played on, 128 answered 2, 71 popped twice, and all 1,575 fields carried agree, walking on's strides among them. Older traces without the pops are not held on them; nor yet one petting tick in 3,000, a count one short. Across into another state of its own: the new state is entered within the handler that asks for it, at a depth the trace did not write, so the first tick written of it came after its entering -- which writes over what the tick before wrote, readying a walk putting its marker in `+0x35a6` -- and the cat had mostly gone on by then (readying a walk, 22, at once walking on, 23). So the driver writes each such entering too: an `entered` line, its state and fields as it is entered -- what the tick that asked for it wrote -- and an `enteredafter` line, its fields after (a line of its own, for an entering may enter another, readying a walk walking on, written between). Held against them, what the asking tick wrote agrees: 16 fields scared and 2 pouncing over 2,000 frames each, most asking ticks writing none of those held.

## On its own

- [[inferred]] `CatzPet` (`src/behaviour/catz-pet.ts`) runs the reimplemented pieces together without the engine: each tick its extreme situations, then -- its script playing on -- what its state writes every tick (`catzStateBetween`), or -- its script done -- its state's tick (`catzStateTick`; the moment's, `catzMomentTick`), and what it asks: actions played, states entered (their entering run at once; looking at things filling what it is curious about, `catzCuriosities`; the moment begun by `catzMomentBegin` from its readying), global states begun (`catzGlobalBegin`, 6, 8 and 9). What stands in for the engine is said so and counted: how long an action plays is given; walking moves straight to what it walks to, four a tick, finding it within 8; the pen is empty -- its things on the shelf, the cursor still at the stage's lower middle -- and the cat on the floor (placed 1); in its moment the ticks to its next look count down between its scripts, its look left to its next lingering; and a global state whose beginning is not reimplemented is idled instead.
- [[measured]] Every global state it begins, `catzGlobalBegin` begins: grooming (13) and affectionate (14), which it once began itself, among them -- affectionate as read out (its independence effect 15, `SetBehaviorEffect` 2; no sound of its own, `+0x3570` -1, kept as petting keeps it; walking to the cursor, 22, `+0x35a6` 0x17), and as the engine does for 20 cats in the playpen harness. The driver's cases each begin from where the last left the cat -- its place and its frame, which walking's cases pop on -- so 20 affectionate cases added to the global ones at first moved the walking cases that came after them; the driver now runs its local cases before its global ones, and cases added after the last of either move nothing held.
- [[measured]] Headless, with the playpen cat's brains and each action ten ticks, over 5,000 ticks it runs without fault through 15 states: looking at things 3,687 ticks, its moment 495, walking 424, passing the time 142, stalking, pouncing 85 and swatting the cursor 23, resting, dozing and waking, grooming. The engine's cat over the 2,000 frames' day looked at things 662 ticks, played 348, was affectionate 241 and groomed 238: the pet idles more, its toys and food on the shelf and the mouse in its hole, and the global states it asks of them idled instead. The way there found the stand-ins wanting, each in turn: walking needed what it walks to answered (the cursor, its marker), found, and the cat on the floor (placed 1) to arrive; the cursor and marker never on the shelf, or it aligns to them only from beside them; looking at things needed what it is curious about filled, and its look counted down every tick; stalking needed to face its prey and creep, pouncing a crouch; and stalking's entering, not reimplemented, run as a tick, pounced again within its tick.
- [[measured]] With its own scripts (`test/oracle/catz_pet_test.ts`): each action pushed played out by `catzTimeline` from the pose it is in -- as long as its frames shown, and leaving it in its script's pose (`to`, `+0x1500`), which then stands no more in for a stalking crouch -- and the user moving the cursor every 300 ticks, over 5,000 ticks it runs without fault, 134 actions some 41 ticks each: looking at things 1,750 ticks, asleep 1,705, passing the time 478, walking 323, tired 253, swatting 214, stalking 121, its moment 109, pouncing 28. A still cursor beside it it swats for ever, as a cat would that nobody moved the mouse for: its paw missing it by how far the cursor is (standing in for `GetSwatMissDistance`), it swats again while near, tired to nothing.

- [[measured]] In the Catz viewer (`cats.html`, Live): the pet ten ticks a second, as the engine runs, each action it pushes played out by its script (`catzTimeline`, the cat's fudgers along) from the pose it is in, a frame a tick, the cat drawn where it stands (its walk moved straight, not yet by its frames), the mouse over the stage its cursor. A new state drops what was left of the old: the viewer keeps as many frames as the pet has left to play (`playing`). It is placed by its frames on a stage of 640 by 480, the engine's own pixels: each frame queued placed as `PopScript` places it (`placeCatzTick`, a reference frame glued and turned, walks glued), the cat drawn at its rectangle; each walk steered each tick and the pet given its point, the middle of its rectangle (as `GetTargetPt` answers for the cat, measured on the traces: half its width and height in, to a pixel), its rectangle, and which way it faces, its rotation as drawn (`+0x688`), its own straight walk off. It is played and placed by `CatzBody` (`src/behaviour/catz-body.ts`), as the headless test of it (`test/oracle/catz_body_test.ts`) does too: the timeline hands each step over as it is done (`done`), a reference frame as it is popped and a shown one once its fudgers have moved on, and the body places each before the next is aimed -- so each tick of a walk is aimed as the engine aims it, from the rectangle the tick before left, the new frame turned as it now is (`catzAimAt`), and what it walks to looked for in the box ahead of its head that its steering words size (`catzFocusRect`, `catzInside`), the pet's `found` that. A state entered drops the frames still to come (`dropped`): the body is put back where the frame shown left it, its fudgers, pose and blink as they were then. On six seeds of 6,000 ticks each, every walk ended found in its box -- most in two or three strides, those come at from below the user's way, as it must face to be there, running on past (0x254, its steering stopped) and coming round; and stalking resolves, pouncing, where it had stalked 4,900 ticks of 6,000. Before this, aiming each tick from a placement kept within `aim` with reference frames left out had stood it turning on the spot: the stride after finding what it walks to while not facing the user is pushed with its steering stopped, and the viewer had aimed it regardless. Its brains are its breed's, made as the engine makes them (`initCatzBrains`, from the breed file's factors, its age the kitten slider's) and drawn back toward their centres by the engine's clock (milliseconds over 17: some 5.9 a tick), the pet's own changes -- energy, fullness -- kept between. On the CD's Catz, two minutes ran without fault, ten ticks a second: looking at things, its moment, grooming -- one script of 558 frames, nearly a minute -- and passing the time, whose scripts run to 150 ticks each, one time in two going on after.

- [[inferred]] Picked up in the viewer: the pet runs the scruff (`catzScruff`) first of its reflexes, from the viewer's button as the engine has it: pressed (`PetParams` `+0xa`) the tick it went down, held (`+0xc`) after. With the cursor within 100 of its ball 2 where it is drawn, it dangles (33). Dangling asks every tick whether the button was let go (`DoDangling`), so the pet runs it mid-script with its script not done (`done` false), as swatting, and it falls (34) once let go. Each tick of dangling, the body is hung from the cursor by its ball 0x24 (`CatzBody.hang`). In the viewer a press on the cat picks it up and puts nothing in the pen: held while the cursor rose, it dangled under it, and let go it fell and went on into its moment.

- [[inferred]] The cursor wiggled: the pet runs `catzCursorWiggling` after seeing the mouse, as the engine orders its reflexes, from where the cursor is each tick. Ten or more changes of way among 20 ticks, it is scared of what the user holds (the cursor, 0x17), plays with it or attacks it. Else, under half its energy, it readies its moment (`PreMoment` 6, 0x93, watching), turns its head to the cursor (3000) and has it (8). Its `+0x33b8` (the cursor kept one way through its last look) is the mouse's: with it set the cursor never troubles the mouse. In the viewer, the pointer wiggled by the cat had it leave its moment to attack it (22 in global 1).

- [[read out]] Where the playpen's messages come from: a sprite notified of another (`XApt::InitNotifyArray`, `AddNotify`; `InitPetModule` has the cat notified of 0x1d's own and of 0x1b) is told of its condition or state changing through `XApt::ParentNotify` (seg2:28ed, an `XApt` slot), which passes kind, condition, state and a flag to each notified sprite's slot `0x58`. For the cat that is `CatSprite::ChildRespond` (seg16:3e3e), which names them (`GetToyString`, `GetCondString`, `GetStateString`) and calls a responder by kind: `RespondToFood`, `RespondToWaterBowl`, `RespondToSprayBottle`, `RespondToFleaSpray`, `RespondToMouseHole`, `RespondToBottle` and the rest (seg16:4040 to 4590). Each leaves its message (`+0x33c0`): with the condition 1 (the water bowl 1 to 3) and the flag set, the first of its pair (food 1, water 3, the spray 0xf), or the Adoption Kit's own (0x27, 0x28, 0x1d) where `PetParams` `+0xc4` is set; with the state 1, the second (2, 4, 0x10). `HandleCatAptMessages` (seg23:13ed) reads and clears it. A thing's condition (`+0x1da`, the last at `+0x1dc`) and state (`+0x1d8`) are kept by `XApt::SetCond` (seg2:0092) and `SetState` (seg2:0465). Which are read out below.

- [[read out]] The dog (kin 0x1a, `DogSprite`, seg21; its files in `PTZFILES/DOG`) is only a visitor. `InitDogSprite`, `Update` (`XApt::Update` and its draw rectangle), `Draw` and `RunAcrossScreen` (seg21:052f, its slot `0x110`) are all it has. Running across, it lets go (its slot `0x9c` 2), plays its run (action 1) fifty times (its words 0x7549 1 50, `playAction1`), and is put off the stage's left or right by `rand() % 2` -- its width and 50 beyond the edge, facing 0x40 or -0x40 (`+0x688`), halfway down the area (`PetParams` `+0xb2` and `ds:2ba8`) -- and shown. Its one caller is `PetModule::DoDrawFrame` (seg34:3942), only where the engine's debugging is on (`XApex::theirWantDebugStuff`) and the B and R keys are held down together. So in the playpen as it ships the dog never comes; the cat's chasing it (global 16 with 0x1a) is for that visit.

- [[inferred]] The playpen's commands' messages live: `CatzPet.leave` leaves one. In the viewer, the browser's window resized leaves 19, as command 0x7f1 does (outside the Kit), and a Camera button 21, as 0x7f2 does, with its sound first (0x8f, `CAMERA.WAV`, seg12:0e2a; played where Sound is on). Resized, the cat looked about the window (20 in global 20).
