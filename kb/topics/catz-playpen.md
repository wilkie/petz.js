Part of [[topic:catz-behaviour]], how a Catz cat behaves: its addresses are CATZDLL.DLL's, `seg:offset`, segments in decimal. "Above" and "below" may mean that page.

## The pen's things

- [[read out]] The playpen's sprites are made in `PetModule::InitPetModule` (seg34:0e68 on), each added to the tree by its kind (`XApt::AddApt`): the dialog 0x1e, the basket 0x18 and its top 0x19, the cat 0x1d, the shelf 0x22, the menus 0x20 and 0x21, help 0x12, the mouse 0x1b, the marker (a `UtilitySprite`) 0x16, the dog 0x1a, the cursor 0x17, the cloud 0x10, text 0x11, the dream 0x13, the mist 0x14 and the fog 0x15. The shelf's things are made as its definition is read (`XApt::ReadNormal`, seg3:155a on), each kind by its own class: 1 the paint bucket, 2 the spray bottle, 3 the flea spray, 4 the catnip, 5 the Cat Dancer, 6 the yarn ball, 7 the jingle ball, 8 the cheese, 9 the squeaky toy, 0xa the hair brush, 0xb the fish treat, 0xc the food bowl, 0xd the water bowl, 0xe the mouse hole and 0xf the milk bottle. (So 0xe, the mouse hole, is what attacking walks to and runs to; and a piece of food, 8, is the cheese.)
- [[read out]] The sprites' definitions are text, CATZDLL.DLL's resources of type 32513 (1000 the shelf and its things, 2000 the basket, 3000 its top, 4000 the dream, 5000 the fog, 6000 the mist): each a name twice, its rectangle and numbers, and its frames by the id of a bitmap (type 2; type 32514 has bitmaps of the same ids). A kind's are `kind × 1000` on the shelf and `kind × 1000 + 200` in the pen, and more from `+ 220` and `+ 400`. The demo's DLL has those of the paint bucket, the spray bottle, the Cat Dancer (its lure at 5400), the yarn ball, the jingle ball, the cheese, the squeaky toy and the mouse hole (14000), but not of the flea spray, the catnip, the hair brush, the fish treat or the bowls, which its shelf names. Its pictures have 256 colours, bright green at index 11.
- [[read out]] A thing's definition in the shelf's (1000) is its name, its label and eleven numbers (its rectangle first), then its conditions. Each condition has a header (the condition, how many sets, its grab rectangle). Each set has a header (its index, whether it loops, how many frames). Each frame has a header (its index, how many pictures). Each picture is `3`, a rectangle and a bitmap's id. Set 0 is the thing at rest; the others are its states' animations. The things are the last fifteen, kinds 1 to 15 in order: the mouse hole's bitmaps are 17000 on and the bottle's 14000, so their ids don't give their kinds. The food bowl at rest is 12000 on the shelf (0) and 12200 in the pen (1), with 12220 to 12225 its states' frames; 2 to 8 are 12400 to 12406, each with 12410 to 12416 over it, full to empty. The catnip's 2 to 7 are 4400 to 4405. The water bowl's 2 is 13600 and 13601, and its 3 has five frames (13400 to 13414), lapping. The retail game keeps most of the things' bitmaps in CATZREZX.DLL (type 2, by the same ids); the demo's CATZDLL.DLL has its few itself.
- [[read out]] A thing's sets past rest are its states' animations: set n plays while it is in state n (`XApt::SetState`). In the pen (condition 1), set 1 is its landing as it is let go, played once (its loop flag 0): the paint bucket 4 frames, the spray bottle 7, the flea spray 5, the catnip 14, the cheese 7, the squeaky toy 15, the hair brush 7, the treat 5, the food bowl 6 and the water bowl 5. Set 3 is how it looks held and dragged: one picture for most, and for the catnip 12 frames looping (flag 1).
- [[inferred]] The viewer plays them: each notice of a thing's state starts that state's set, a frame a tick, round again where it loops, else back at rest. So a thing let go, struck or taken from the user lands, and one dragged shows as held. The catnip put in the pen plays its landing.
- [[read out]] A thing's target point (`XApt::GetTargetPt`, seg2:172a) is the middle of its target rectangle, halves rounded toward 0. That rectangle (`GetTargetRect`, seg2:1780) is the thing's condition's rectangle (`+0x1c4` to `+0x1ca`, its definition's condition header), from its draw rectangle's corner. So the food bowl's point in the pen is (40, 65) of its 85 by 91 picture, (7, 48) to (74, 83)'s middle. On the closed shelf a thing's rectangle is taken otherwise (seg2:17a0 on, not read out).
- [[inferred]] `parseAptThings` and `aptShelfKinds` (`src/formats/apt.ts`) read them; `test/oracle/catz_apt_test.ts` holds the fifteen and their pictures by condition. The viewer reads CATZREZX.DLL where there is one (the dev server now serves it), so on the retail drive the bowls, the catnip and the rest have their pictures in the pen. Each is drawn with its condition's target point on its point, and a thing in a condition of its own by its set 0's first frame. `?full=N` starts the cat at that fullness: at 5 it ate, and the bowl went from full (2) to empty (8), each step drawn.
- [[read out]] A toy loose in the pen moves by the playpen's physics (`XApt::ToyPhysics`, seg29:0140, CATZDLL ordinal 678), each tick: in the pen (`+0x1da`, or the shelf itself, kind 0x22), not held (`+0x1d8` 0 or 1), with some friction (`+0x21a`) and `+0x220`, and moving (`+0x13a`, `+0x13c`), it makes its rolling sound and moves by its speed from its target point; its speed keeps its friction's share (over 100, `ds:0x382`, a single), rounded toward 0. Past the pen's left, top, right or bottom edge -- its window's client rectangle less `PetParams` `+0xd8` and `+0xda`, less half its grab rectangle (`GetGrabRect`, space 1) -- it is put at the edge, and that way its speed turns back, its rebound's share (`GetReboundTrackSpeed`: `+0x21c`, or, that under 0, its friction less 10), with its bouncing sound. Else it stops: its speed 0, `+0x23c` 0. Its last five target points are kept (`+0x72` on), a throw's speed made of the last two (`SetupTrackSpeed`, within 50). The friction is its definition's eighth number after its rectangle -- 50 for most, 70 the squeaky toy, 92 the yarn ball, 95 the jingle ball -- the yarn ball's own `Init` making it and its rebound 100. The cheese and the squeaky toy are moved so; the yarn ball counts its rolling frame (`+0x25e`, 0 to 5) first (`YarnBallSprite::ToyPhysics`, 871); the Cat Dancer is not moved so at all: its `ToyPhysics` (seg20:24aa, ordinal 550) only keeps the cursor's last five points (`PetParams` `+0x5a`, into `+0x72` on) and its `SetSpeed` (548) only keeps the speed given (`+0x136`), its spring its own `Update` (seg20:1214), not read out. Moving, every toy makes its rolling sound (`XApt::PlayRollSound`, seg29:0073): where its count (`+0x23c`) is 0 it plays it (`XPlayStereoSound`, a draw of C's `rand` for its loudness), and counts on to 11 and round again -- so every twelfth tick rolling draws C's `rand`; stopped, the count is 0. Its bouncing sound (`PlayBounceSound`) plays nothing.
- [[read out]] The jingle ball moves by its own (`JingleBallSprite::ToyPhysics`, seg32:0a10, ordinal 747), its speed singles (`+0x242`, `+0x246`, made of a whole speed by its `SetSpeed`, 748): rolling, its sound so, where the square of its speed, rounded toward 0, is over 6; it moves by its speed's whole parts (rounded toward 0), and its speed is kept at (400 less `PetParams` `+0x138`) over 400 (`ds:0x3c8`), a single; within 0.01 either way (`ds:0x3cc`, `0x3d4`), 0. Past an edge -- left, top, right, bottom, in turn -- it is put at the edge and sent off again: the size of its speed the root of its whole parts' squares; its way that speed's turned back on that axis (`atan2`), in degrees (180, `ds:0x3dc`, over π, `0x3e0`), turned by `PetParams` `+0x140` less `rand() % (2 × +0x140 + 1)` degrees, so each bounce a draw of C's `rand`. On the playpen's parameters `+0x138` is 13 and `+0x140` 64: a bounce turns it up to 64 degrees either way.

- [[read out]] Struck (`XApt::StrikeToy`, seg29:073c, ordinal 676), not the mouse, a toy is given a speed each way within 50 (its slot `0x4c`), and a sound (`0x48`); the Cat Dancer otherwise. A swat strikes (`DoCatSwatting`, seg29:0eee on) at the cat's rotation plus `rand() % 50` on its side 0, less it on its other, made shortest, and turned about for the shelf's: 100 that way (seg48:1934: across less the sine, down the cosine, of the engine's tables -- `ds:0x4610` the sines, `0x460c` the cosines, made with the true π, seg48:1410 -- each times 100 over 256, rounded toward 0), all but the cursor, which it moves, and what is on the closed shelf, knocked through the shelf.
- [[measured]] `catzToyTick` and `catzJingleTick` (`src/behaviour/catz-toys.ts`) move the toys as the engine does at every tick of 24 struck in the playpen harness -- the cheese, the squeaky toy, the yarn ball (with its own physics) and the jingle ball, each at full speed four ways and two aslant, 40 ticks each: 960 ticks, bouncing off every edge, their speed falling as each friction has it, and the jingle ball's speed to the bit, its bounces turned by C's `rand` from where the strike left it, its rolling sound's draws among them (its count kept from case to case).
- [[inferred]] The pet strikes so (`strike`), its swat cued to strike (cue 5, the body's shown frame's cues) as the frame is popped, mid-script; and the viewer moves what it struck by the playpen's physics, the jingle ball by its own, the Cat Dancer not at all; their sounds draw the cat's own C `rand`, as the engine's do. Standing in, a toy on the shelf (along the stage's top, standing in) is out of its paw's reach (`GetSwatMissDistance` far), as the shelf is but by jumping; its prey's place is where it is, on the shelf or in the pen.
- [[inferred]] The viewer draws the pen's things put in it with their pictures in the pen, bright green left out, about their points, the cat in front; those the demo has none of, as markers.

## The mouse

- [[read out]] The playpen's mouse (`MouseSprite`, seg26 and 27; kin 0x1b) has two machines, as the cat does. Its global one (`+0x257a`; names in resource 4000) is in its hole (1), exploring (2) or fleeing (3). Its own (`+0x2518`, its number at `+0x2552`; names in resource 2000) is waiting in its hole (1), might it come out (2), walking (3), sniffing what it came for (4), looking around (5), dangling (6), after dangling (7) or playing dead (8). Each tick (`MouseSprite::Update`, seg27:0f21) its four brains drift by the clock, then come its scruff and its CatNapz size, then the global handler and then its own. Nothing in its own code starts it: `PetModule::InitPetModule` (seg34:24ad) puts it in its hole (global 1). Most handlers' entering falls through into their tick.
- [[read out]] Its brains (`+0x257e` on, 0x52 apart), by how they are used: restless (0, its wish to come out), fear (1), fullness (2) and curiosity (3). `InitBrain` (seg27:0bc0) takes their centres from MOUSE.LNZ (a draw each) and their values from the saved profile, then makes its fear 50 to 99 (a fifth draw). Afraid (fear 50 or more), it is always in danger (`IsMouseInDanger`, seg26:16d5). Otherwise it is in danger with the cat within 200, the cat's age (brain 11) 30 or more, and the cat in neither global state 9 nor 10; then its fear is made 50 to 99 again, a draw. A sprite is too close for comfort (`IsSpriteTooCloseForComfort`, seg26:15f3) within `MapValue(100, 150, fear)`, from where the mouse is as it asks. That is after any pop of its script that tick, which places it by its new frame. The cursor never is where the cat's `+0x33b8` is set.
- [[read out]] Waiting (1), it decides every 101 ticks, unless the cat is in global state 0x15. It is put at its hole, restless 5 more, and with the cheese off the shelf within 100, 10 less afraid and 5 less full. Then it picks what it will go for (`+0x26d8`): the cheese (8) at `rand() % 101` under what its fullness leaves of 100; else what it is curious about (`GetNextCuriosity`, seg26:1b28: up to ten draws of `rand() % 15` into a table of kinds, the cat for 13 and 14, until one is off the shelf, the cursor where none is) at one under its curiosity; else nothing. It comes out (2) at a draw under its restlessness, where its hole is not covered (`PetParams` `+0xfe`, or the shelf closed) and the cat is not in 0x15, it goes for something, a draw under what its fear leaves of 100, it is not in danger, and neither the cat nor the cursor is too close.
- [[read out]] Might it come out (2): shown at its hole, it pushes 0x16, and clears the cat's `+0x33bc`, that it may see it again. After 20 ticks, then after `rand() % (fear + 10)`:
  - in danger, or with the cat too close, or the cursor too close and not holding the cheese, it goes back in (1);
  - else, its script done once, at a draw under 80, it explores (global 2);
  - else one time in what its curiosity says, with nothing layered, it layers 0x15.
- [[read out]] Walking (3) aims its rotation each tick along a sum of ways (`ModifyVector`, `SetLength`: `atan2`, the engine's cosine and sine times a length over 256). Toward what it goes for, at its hunger for the cheese, its fear for its hole (0xe), else its curiosity. Away from the cat and the cursor where either is too close, twice its fear each; threatened, toward its goal only at a draw under what its fear leaves of 100. Its script done:
  - after 30 strides, not going home, it looks around for something new (5), curious (50 or more), else goes home;
  - one time in two with nothing layered, it layers 0x15;
  - touching its hole (`CollisionDetect`), it is in it (global 1); touching anything else off the shelf and unthreatened, it sniffs (4);
  - not touching, not going home, and the cheese more than 100 off, at one draw in five it looks around (5);
  - else another stride (1; 0x1e going home), 1 less restless.

  Exploring (global 2), in danger it flees (global 3, which makes for its hole); restless under 10, it goes home. Sniffing, looking around, dangling (from the cursor, or the cat, 2 to 4 times before it plays dead), after dangling and playing dead are read out in the model's comments.

- [[measured]] The pen harness follows the mouse too (`TRACE.TXT`'s 33554432, `TRACE_MICE=1`). It writes each dispatch of either machine, before and after (`mtick`, `mticked`: the seed and its fields and brains, the cat's point, global state, age and `+0x33b8`, the cursor and cheese, the shelf, its layered frames left), each pop of its script (`mpop`: seed, answer, seed after, and its place), and each `CollisionDetect` answer (`mcollide`). It also makes a waiting mouse restless (100) and unafraid (20) every 100 frames; else, made 50 to 99 afraid, it never comes out. Over 1,500 frames so, it came out curious about the cat, explored some 750 ticks, walking and looking around, went home, and came out twice more.
- [[measured]] `CatzMouse` (`src/behaviour/catz-mouse.ts`) holds to all 3,002 of the mouse's dispatches in that day (`test/harness/catzmouse_test.ts`): its `rand`, states, count, target, holder, waits and brains after each. Each dispatch starts from the engine's fields, its script's pops answering and drawing as the engine's did, and its collisions answered by the engine. A second day (`TRACE_MICE=2`, `TRACE.TXT`'s 67108864) puts the cheese by its hole every 500 frames and, at 300 into each, with the mouse out, has the user pick it up by the scruff for 40 frames. The mouse's dangling needs the button down (`+0xa`) for as long as it hangs, as the cat's scruff does not. Over 1,500 frames it was picked up once while walking (`HandleScruff`: dangling, 6, held by the cursor, 0x17). It hung 41 ticks, after dangling (7) explored, and was in danger, so it fled (global 3) home. The model holds all 2,960 of that day's dispatches but the one it was let go in, which needs the button and its `+0xfc`, now written. With the button and `+0xfc` written, it holds that tick too. Made hungry as it waits (fullness 10), the mouse went for the cheese and sniffed it (4, 73 ticks), and the model holds those ticks. Then the cat's catch was stood in for: at 450 of each 500, out, its holder made the cat (0x1d, as `DoCatVictimCaptured` makes it, seg19:08b9) and its fear 60, that it is in danger. It fled, found itself held and dangled from the cat (50 ticks), until let go at 499. The model holds all 2,940 dispatches of that day. Dangling from the cat, it plays dead only after 2 to 4 of its scripts are done, so the catch now comes at 380. Over 1,500 frames so, it dangled from the cat twice and played dead (8) each time, 52 and 61 ticks, till let go; after dangling it explored, and later went home. The model holds all 2,887 dispatches of that day. Every state the mouse has, its 8 own and its 3 global, is now held against the engine. Not yet: its brains' drift by the clock, its scruff (`HandleScruff`, before its machines) and its CatNapz size.
- [[measured]] Its size: `InitMouseSprite` makes its pet and ball scales (`+0x684`, `+0x68e`, the drawn `BallState`'s `+0xc` and `+0x16`) 200, where a cat takes its LNZ's default scales (`+0x2cc6`, `+0x2cc8`, 128 for the Clown and the Hunter); MOUSE.LNZ's own, 700 and 1000, are not used. The trace now writes its rectangle and frame (`mrect`). Placed by `CatzPlacer` at scales of 200, its frame 208 is 47 by 42, as the engine's is in 2,754 of 6,164 rectangles written. Its walk (action 1) cycles frames 195 to 200 in both. The engine's walk moves its target point some 6.9 a tick, much of it in jumps where it turns sharply (34 across from 195 to 196, turning 31). Turning reshapes its rectangle, so a walk measured without its steering moves it less (4). With the mouse out, the cat swatted at it (`+0x35a4` 0x1b), and two of its ticks first disagreed. A swat's strike can be cued in the middle of its script, and there `DoCatSwatting` strikes and stops: only a done script goes on to choose and count a swat (seg29:113d tests `PopScript`'s 1). The model went on regardless. It stops now where the trace says its script was not done (`done`). The pet runs only `catzStrike` mid-script anyway. Both mouse days' cats now agree: 957 and 863 fields carried.

- [[inferred]] Alive (`CatzMouseLive`, `src/behaviour/catz-mouse-live.ts`): its machines played by a body of its own (`CatzBody`, MOUSE.BHD's frames, MOUSE.LNZ, MOUSE.SCP's 36 scripts in 11 positions, neutral 5, at scales of 200). Its brains are made as `InitBrain` makes them from MOUSE.LNZ's `[Default Factors]` (restless 80, fear 15, fullness 50 and curiosity 50, each with a spread and a rate). Every brain draws, even one without a spread, and then its fear is made 50 to 99; the clock draws them back to their centres. Popping its script shows a frame and answers done once nothing pushed is left. Pushed while walking, its rotation's fudger is aimed at the heading its handler last aimed (its rate 5000). Waiting, it is put at its hole. Its collisions are the 40-square box about its ball 9, a quarter of the diagonal ahead (`catzFocusRect` with a ball), against the cursor within 15, its hole within 5, or another thing within 20 (standing in for that thing's rectangle). What layers over its script is not played. Headless (`test/oracle/catz_mouse_live_test.ts`), with a kitten asleep at the far side, it comes out, explores as far as 500 from its hole and goes home on two days in three. On the third, its fear drew back from where it began only to 58 in ten minutes, and at 50 or more it never comes out.
- [[inferred]] In the Catz viewer (Live), it lives in a hole at the floor's lower left, drawn by its own file at scales of 200 (`drawPet`'s `scales`). With the cat grown and within 200, it is in danger, and its fear goes back to 50 to 99. It also stays in with the cursor where Live begins, within its comfort of the hole. `?mouse=bold` starts it unafraid (20) and restless (100). With a kitten and the cursor away, it came out within 40 seconds and explored the pen. - [[inferred]] The cat sees it. `CatzPet` runs `catzSeesMouse` as its first reflex, before extreme situations. Out of its hole, within the stage, not reacted to since its global state changed (`+0x33bc`): reacting, the mouse is what it plays with (`+0x35a4` 0x1b), a global state begins (attacking, playing, lazily watching, snubbing or scared), and `+0x33bc` is 1 after. A new global state clears it (`GlobalCatStateMachine::NewState`), and so does the mouse coming out (`DoMouseMightComeOut`). The mouse's state and point are its world's `mouseState`, its prey's and what it walks to. Swatting's "the mouse running" is the mouse back in its hole: its state is 1 (seg29:1580).
- [[read out]] A catch: a pounce cued to catch (2) has the mouse dodge unless `rand() % 101` is at or over 100 less half its age, or, with an age, another draw reaches 70 (seg14:0fbc). Not dodged, `IsSpriteCaught` (seg14:1b63) asks whether the box of its balls 0x16 and 0x17 (its paws), 70 wider and 40 taller each way (`XInsetRect` by -70, -40), meets the prey's rectangle. If so, and the prey is off the shelf, it is held by ball 0x42 (slots `0xf0`, `0xf8`: `+0xee0`). A dive's catch is the one draw at or over 100 less half its age. Its victim's handler stills it, its fear 100. Let go, it puts it down with the mouse's holder the cat (0x1d, seg19:08b9), which the mouse's fleeing finds: it dangles and plays dead. Its holder goes back to 0 where the cat is itself picked up (`DoDangling`, seg23:4285), or leaves having its victim (global 2). Global 2 (`DoGSHasVictim`, seg33:14c0), which putting the mouse down begins, gives no effects of its own and carries it (7, `DoCatCarryVictim`: its script done, it lets go and makes ready to walk, 22). Each tick, with a double-click, it readies its moment, watching, and has it. Leaving, it lets go of what it holds (its slot `0xfc`) and, the mouse held by the cat to its mind, frees it. `catzGlobalBegin`, `catzGlobalTick` and the pet do so (read out).
- [[inferred]] The model has the pounce and dive answer the catch (`caught`), from the draws and the reach its world gives (`inReach`, as the viewer works it from the cat's paws and the mouse's rectangle). The pet keeps it (`+0xee0`) and tells the mouse (`mouseHeld`): caught, carried in the cat's mouth; stilled; put down; free. A kitten never catches: the pounce's catch needs an age. The cue to catch comes in the middle of the pounce's script, and the handler checks it each tick (seg14:0f70), going on only with its script done (seg14:10b7), as swatting does. So the pet runs the pounce or dive on a tick cued 2 mid-script, with its script not done (`done` false), and keeps any catch. Leaving its victim's states unless let go, the cat throws the mouse (`StrikeToy`; the throw's motion is not modelled), and it is free, stilled with fear 100, so it flees. With the mouse unafraid and the cat aged 20 (`test/oracle/catz_body_test.ts`, four days of 6,000 ticks, under 30 so the mouse is not in danger of it), the cat went after the mouse most of each day. It was cued to catch it in reach 2 to 6 times a day and caught it on two days, holding it as its victim (52 and 9 ticks) before throwing it; the mouse fled home. It snubbed it too (global 11). `DoGSSnub` (seg30:17e4), entering, gives no effects of its own (the global machine's `NewState` makes them 0) and snubs what it plays with (0x50, `DoCatSnubObject`, modelled already); leaving, its head no longer looks away (`+0x1514` 0, not modelled); its tick does nothing. `catzGlobalBegin` begins it so (read out, not held against the harness), and the pet no longer stands in for anything over those four days. In the viewer it stalked it. The pet aims a pounce's and a dive's leap at its prey (`+0x35a4`'s point, the mouse's among them), not at what it walks to. The viewer puts the mouse hole (0xe) in the pet's pen at the mouse's hole, so attacking or sniffing it goes there. A sprite is kept only touching the area (`keepCatzTouching`), so a cat or mouse going for something by the stage's edge may stand almost wholly past it, as the engine allows: the harness's area is the whole window too.
