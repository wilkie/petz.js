## CatNapz

- [[read out]] CatNapz is global state 21 (`DoGSCatNapz`, seg18:0000). Entering, the cloud's offset (`+0x35f0`, `+0x35f2`) is 0; one time in ten (`rand() % 101` under 10) it chases the dog (0x46, `DoCatStartDogChase`); else `+0x35ee` is 0 and, one time in two, it pushes 0x2a6, has its energy put at 20 (`+0x302c`'s slot `0x10`), its slot `0x238` 0, `+0x35a6` 0, and walks (22); else it lies down tired (26). Leaving, its dream sprite (kin 0x13), cloud (kin 0x10) and kin 0x1a are put away (slot `0x7c`), `+0x3502` 100, and its slot `0x3a8` and brains' `+0x3360` and `+0x1f6` are set back. Its cloud (`CloudSprite`, seg32) and dream sprite (`DreamSprite`, seg32) are made by `InitPetModule` with the rest, so they are there in the playpen.
- [[inferred]] The pet naps (`CatzPet.nap`, beginning global 21 as `catzGlobalBegin` now has it, read out: no effects; one time in ten the dog's chase, 70; else `+0x35ee` 0 and one time in two 0x2a6, energy 20, `+0x35a6` 0 and walking, else lying down tired). It keeps the dream (its phase, kind, `+0x35d8` and the float's doubles) as the states write it, runs starting a dream (65) and the dream's mouse (68) every tick, as their handlers pop and ask, and draws the bad dream's breed (cue 7, `LoadRandomLinezFile`: one time in two `rand() % 6`, else `rand() % 9`, into `+0x35ee`). Standing in for what the screen saver's other scripts do, as the harness's driver does, are four things: the cat's cue to dream on phase 0's second tick; the cloud's that it has come, 283 ticks into phase 1, as the engine's own cloud comes (where it has no cloud of its own, below); and the dog and the dream's mouse each setting off 200 left of the stage and running across, 10 and 8 a tick.
- [[inferred]] In the viewer (Live) a Nap button puts the cat to nap (`nap`). Floating (65's phase 3), the cat is put where the float has it each tick (`moveTo` its goal, as the engine sets its target point). Once: tired, it walked, started a dream, floated in from (366, 221) to about the stage's middle, and dreamed well, its eyes shut, without error. (Its dream's pictures and mouse: below.)
- [[read out]] The dream sprite (kin 0x13) is a definition of its own (CATZDLL type 32513, 4000, `dream`): 200 by 200, by what it dreams of (`+0x35ec`, `DreamSprite::SetDream`) one of the pen's things -- 0 the catnip (4200), 1 the yarn (6200), 2 the jingle ball (7200), 3 the cheese (8200), 4 the squeaky toy (9200), 5 the treat (11200), 6 the food bowl (12200), 7 the spray bottle (2200), 8 the hair brush (10200) -- each centred on its target point (about (100, 100)). Dreaming well, cued (7), it is put with its target point the cat's less the cat's height (`+0x33c6`) and shown; for 10, the pen's mouse is put there instead, let go, pushes 0x22 and is shown. Cued (6), both are put away. Each dreaming action cues 7 41 frames in, on a reference frame, and 6 60 to 200 frames later. The cloud (`CloudSprite`, seg32) is a scripted sprite with files of its own, not read.
- [[inferred]] The pet keeps what the dream sprite shows and where (`dream.shown`). The viewer draws it over all, by the definition (`parseAptThings`, 4000). For 10 it takes the live mouse out of its hole, its machine paused, plays 0x22 there, and puts it back after. Napped in the browser, a good dream of the mouse showed it dancing above the sleeping cat. The cloud and the dog are drawn too (below).
- [[read out]] Tired in CatNapz (26; seg30:018a), its script done, it is put at 20 energy, lets go (its slot `0x238` 0), walks to nothing (`+0x35a6` 0) and walks (22). The model had kept what it walked to, so a cat that had walked to the shelf walked there again for ever; corrected.
- [[inferred]] The chase hovering at the stage's edge was the model's: its chase ran back at once, not put past the stage nor waiting for the dog, and the walk back, from where the last left it, found its target by frames looked for before (`there`). Now the arrival and the chase put the cat (`place`; the pet's `moveTo`, the body's `moveTo`, which forgets what its frames found), the chase waits for the dog's stand-in to pass (it runs 10 a tick, the way it was put facing), and the walk to the middle sets `+0x35ee` 0xb, ending the chase there. Headless, with the real body, 40 grown cats napped over 6,000 ticks each: every chase ran across, waited off the stage some 70 ticks, ran back, and ended at the middle or tripping, none held at an edge. The dog's own scripts as it goes (7, 1, and the cat's sound 2) are, since, with the dog's own body (below).
- [[measured]] The chase's wait against the saved traces: the chase reads where the dog is after its script is popped in the tick, which moves it, so the replay gives it the dog's place as the next tick begins (`followTrace`'s `dogAfter`). So the 4,000 frames' nap trace -- three chases' ticks where the dog crossed the line within the tick (563 to 570 and more, way 1; -99 to under -100, way 2) and ran back -- and all the other saved traces agree.
- [[measured]] How the dog moves, from the 4,000 frames' nap trace (its target point across, each tick): it stands where it is put through the chase's start and the cat's walks, and moves only in the chase (71), as its script is pushed -- running about 25 to 35 a tick, as its own run (action 1) does at its own scale, and lingering some 70 ticks at 407 to 429, within 200 of the stage's middle (235), barking (7). Past the line (563, way 1; -99, way 2), it and the cat are put 200 past it (670, -199) and it stands again till the cat, run back and put 500 past, waits for it.
- [[inferred]] The dog played by its own files (`CatzDogLive`, `src/behaviour/catz-dog.ts`: `PTZFILES/DOG`, a body of its own at its own scale, playing only what it is pushed). The pet takes it as `dog` (where it is, whether its script is done, push, put, put away) in place of its stand-in, and runs the dog's part of the chase each tick before its own state's (barking three times in ten within 200 of the middle, else the cat's sound 2 drawn, then running on), putting it at the chase's start (200 left, facing -64) and with the cat at each end, and away as the chase ends or CatNapz is left. Headless (`test/oracle/catz_body_test.ts`), 30 grown cats napping 4,000 ticks each: 13 chases, the dog run past the line 33 times and barked 15, the cat never still more than 4 ticks walking in one. In the viewer it is drawn behind the cat: napped till a chase came, the dog ran across after it and back, and the chase ended at the middle, without fault.
- [[measured]] The dog's part of the chase against the engine (`catzDogsPart`, which the pet runs): with `TRACE_VISITORS=1` the driver now hooks the dog's own table (kin 0x1a; its push, slot `0xb8`) and `PopScript` for it, writing each pop (`dpop`: the seed before, what it answered -- bit 0 its script done -- the seed after, and where it is across) and each push (`dogpush`: the action, the seed after); its script's own pushes, as it is popped, come before the pop's line. - [[read out]] The cloud (kin 0x10, `CloudSprite`, seg32; its files in `PTZFILES/CLOUD`). - [[measured]] With `TRACE_NAP=1` (`TRACE.TXT`'s 512) the driver puts the cat in CatNapz on every tenth tick of looking at things, passing the time, its moment, walking on or grooming (`CATZTRACE-NAP.OUT`). The cat's scripts never cue it to dream (6): 0xb3 and 0xd5, and what they play, cue nothing, so whatever cues it is outside the playpen (the screen saver, `CATNAPZ.DLL`, which runs a pet module of its own). So the driver cues the cat (6) on its second tick in phase 0, once. It also answered the cloud's cue that it has come (4) after 20 ticks in phase 1, thought then never to come; it does (below). Cued again in every tick, the cat sends the cloud its script over and over, and, the cloud's script stack (`Stack::Push`, seg42:1148, called by `ScriptSprite::PushScript`) runs past its segment, and the run stops with no fault reported: the CPU stays on the store at seg42:1174.
