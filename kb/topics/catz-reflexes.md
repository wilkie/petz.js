## Reflexes

- [[read out]] `CatSprite::Update` calls its reflexes through its own table (base seg16:0x4999, as its relocations give the slots): `0x12c` `HandleBlinking`, `0x148` `HandleAutoBreedSwitch`, `0x138` `HandleCatAptMessages`, `0x144` `HandleKitContextSensitiveText`, `0x120` `HandleScruff`, `0x134` `HandleCatSeesMouse`, and then `0x128` `HandleCursorWiggling`, `0x124` `HandlePetting`, `0x140` `HandlePurring`, `0x130` `HandleCatExtremeSituations` and `0x13c` `HandleMiscLayering` (seg23 each).
- [[read out]] Its gates (seg16:25c0 to 2830): blinking, breed switching and the playpen's messages every tick; in the Kit (`PetParams` `+0xc4`) the Kit's text, and `+0x361e` (the stage under 640 by 400); then in its baskets (73) nothing more; else the scruff; then peeking, ending a peek, climbing out, jumping or dropping in (74 to 78), or on its way to its basket (`+0x35fc`), nothing more; else seeing the mouse and the rest. The pet had none of these gates and gave the scruff no Kit fields (`+0x35fc`, `+0x35fa`, its basket and `+0x35f8` as 0, 0, -1, 0); now it has both. A kitten picked up in the Kit keeps its basket and marks it as been in, and out of it (73 to 76) shows its breed's words. A unit test picks one up as it peeks, and not with `+0x35fc` set.
- [[read out]] Extreme situations (`HandleCatExtremeSituations`, seg23:0972): `+0x358c` counts down a tick at a time; at 0 or under it is 280 again (0x118), `+0x3574` 0, and, `PetParams` `+0xc4` clear, inclined to behaviour 22 (a diet under 10 proper) its behaviour changes (`DoCatBehaviorChange`, slot `0x2b4`) so, 3; else inclined to 23 (over 75) and full (brain 4 at 50 or more), 4.
