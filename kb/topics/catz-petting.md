## Petting

- [[read out]] Petting (global state; `DoGSPetting`, seg28:0000), entering: its slot `0x114` (2, 0xf), `+0x3570` -1, its slot `0x2d0` (`InitPettingModes`: the 40 modes kept at `+0x351c` cleared) and its own state begun (0x31). Leaving, the cursor's script is reset and turned to a neutral pose (`XCursor::ResetStack`, `PushTransitionToNeutral`), its fuzz and eyelids reset (`DefuzzCat`, `ResetEyelids`), its head let go, and kin 0xa, held, set so. Each tick, a double-click (`PetParams` `+0x46`), its moment, watching (8).
- [[read out]] Its petting modes are a history: `SetPettingMode` (slot `0x2d4`) pushes a mode onto the 40 at `+0x351c`, newest first, and `GetPettingModeHistory` (`0x2d8`) answers the one so many back. `IsCatGettingMoreIntimate` (`0x2e4`) is whether the last behaviour-5 of them are all one mode: whether it has settled.
- [[read out]] Where it is petted: `WhichBallBeingPet` (`0x2e8`) asks the cat's ballz (`+0x242`, their slot `0x48`) which ball lies under the cursor's point (`PetParams` `+0x5a`) in its frame, and answers it where it is 0 to 0x42, not 0x41 or 0x42, and `PetParams` `+0x1e` is set, else -1. `WhichRegionBeingPet` (`0x2ec`) maps the ball to a region by a jump table (seg28:1a97): 3 for balls 7 to 11, 14, 15, 24, 27 to 29, 37, 40 and 55 to 62; 4 for 4, 5, 30, 31 and 36; 5 for 16 to 23, 34, 35, 63 and 64; 6 for 0, 1, 41, 42 and 49 to 54; 7 for 43 to 48; 8 for 2, 6, 12, 13, 32, 33, 38 and 39; 9 for 3, 25 and 26; else 0. `GetCursorMovement` (`0x2f0`) is how far the cursor (kin 0x17) is from where it was (`+0x3518`, `+0x351a`), 1 where that is -1. `HasUserStoppedPetting` (`0x2f8`) is true but where the cursor moved, lies in the cat's draw rectangle inset by 10, and one of `PetParams` `+0x1e` to `+0x24` is set.

- [[read out]] What petting writes, read out of seg28 (corrections to the above first):
  - `HasUserStoppedPetting` widens the draw rectangle by 10, rather than insetting it.
  - `GetCursorMovement` tests only `+0x3518` for -1, and writes nothing.
  - **Entering petting (global 7):** `+0x3570` is -1, and the 40 modes are 0.
  - **`SetPettingMode`:** pushes to the front of the 40, dropping the oldest.
  - **`PetRegionSwitch`:** the upsetting regions (1, 2, 5 to 7) push mode 1 before anything else. Otherwise, from beginning, it pushes mode 3 where `+0x3576` is set (awake from resting), else mode 2, and clears `+0x3576`.
  - **Being petted (50), entering:** `+0x34ca` 0; where the cursor was -1; its rate `(trunc((30 - b) / 30 * 5) + 1) * 2` (b behaviour 5; the floats at `ds:0x37a` and `0x37e` are 30 and 5); its count 0; the cursor's action -1; and three times in ten a sound (5). Then its tick, its script not done.
  - **Being petted, each tick:** with under 5 of its script left (`GetStackRemaining`) and `+0x34ca` 1, it stores the cursor's target point as where it was, and `+0x34ca` becomes 0.
  - **Being petted, its script done:**
    - Not stopped, `+0x34ca` becomes 1.
    - The sound wanted (`+0x3572`): with no sound of its own, 1 one time in ten; with one, 1 one time in ten, else 3.
    - Its mode is pushed, moved on or the same. The hairbrush held, 2 is pushed again, and the switch then takes that: not modelled.
    - The cursor's action (`+0x362a`) becomes 7, and 0xc or 0xf in some branches. Its count goes on, and its script is popped again.
  - **May it end (51):** entering, it keeps its mode (`+0x356c`) and tracks the cursor with its head (3000). Its script done, `+0x3572` becomes 4 first of all. Going back to petting, mode 1 is made 2. Ending a mode, it pushes the mode less 1.
  - **After petting (52):** resting at once, it sets `+0x3574` 1.
  - **Purring (`HandlePurring`):** runs in the next tick's `Update` before the state's handler, and turns the sound wanted into a purr.

- [[read out]] When the playpen tells the cat. `ParentNotify` is the `XApt` vtable slot `0x54` (`IsSomethingBeingHeld` 0x20, `IsThisBeingHeld` 0x24). The root's list is the cat (0x1d) and the mouse (0x1b), whose responder is empty. A thing's states are 0 ready, 1 let go, 2 selected, 3 dragged (held), 4 pressed; its conditions 0 on the shelf, 1 in the pen. Five places notify:
  - `SetState` twice: a thing in the pen tells its parent, the root; a thing on its shelf (kinds 1 to 15) tells the root. Both with flag 0.
  - `ShelfToPlaypen` (seg3:3d34; at 3:409e): (kind, 1, its state, 1) where the user takes it out, none where the cat knocks it off (`CatSprite::ShelfToPen`).
  - `DoUpdate` twice, (kind, 1, 3, 1), a locked thing pressed (`+0x1d0` 0; the Adoption Kit's).

  `SetCond`, `UserSetCond` and `CatSetCond` tell no one. The flag (`ChildRespond`'s fourth) is "the user just took it off the shelf", not a distance; the only distance tests are the cat's own (the sprays' box) and dropping on the shelf.

  The mouse's actions on a thing of the pen (its table from resource 32513, 7000):
  - a press sets state 4 then 3, and takes it off the shelf where it is there;
  - its first held frame sets 3 again;
  - let go, it sets 1, put back first where it is over the open shelf;
  - its let-go animation done, 0.

  So the user taking a thing out tells (kind, 1, 3, 1) then, let go, (kind, 1, 1, 0); pressing one in the pen, (kind, 1, 4, 0) and (kind, 1, 3, 0), let go (kind, 1, 1, 0); putting it back, nothing the cat answers (condition 0). A swat (`StrikeToy`) or the cat taking what the user holds (`ForceRelease`) tells (kind, its condition, 1, 0).

  The responders (`catzChildRespond`), in the conditions each answers (the food bowl 1 to 8, the water bowl 1 to 3, catnip 1 to 7, the yarn and jingle balls 1 and 2, the rest 1): taken out, the first of its pair (in the Kit its own, 0x1c to 0x29); then by state, which wins:
  - let go (1): food 2, water 4, the spray 0x10, the flea spray 0x11, catnip 8, a treat 6;
  - pressed (4): the Cat Dancer 0xc, yarn 0xa, jingle 9, squeaky 0xb, a treat 5;
  - dragged (3): the bottle 0xd.
