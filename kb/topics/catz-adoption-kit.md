## In the Adoption Kit

- [[read out]] The Adoption Kit is the module's command 5 (`PetParams` `+0`, which `PetModule::DoDrawFrame` switches on, 1 to 6, a table at seg34:3b6c; 4 is retail): its shelf, buttons and baskets set up (`XApt::SetupAdoptionShelf`, `SetupButtonsForAdoption`, `SetupBasketsForAdoption`) and the cat put in (`SetupCatForAdoption`), global state 22 (`DoGSAdoptionKit`, seg13:0000), which, entering, by `+0x33be` begins it (0x48), climbs out (0x4d), drops in (0x4e) or walks to its basket.
- [[read out]] Beginning it (72; `DoCatInitKit`, seg13:0162): entering, put above the stage's corner, 0x1c4, in a basket (its slot `0xf0`, 0x18), the baskets been in (`+0x3614`, 5) none; its script done, in the baskets (0x49, `+0x35f6` -1). In them (73; `DoCatInBaskets`, seg13:0290): entering, with no basket it takes one (its slots `0x3d0`, `0x3d4`), waiting none (`+0x3502`, `+0x360e`), else it climbs out (0x4c); each tick, waited longer than its wait, it peeks out (0x4a), else waits on; its script done, 0x1c4. Peeking (74; `DoCatPeekFromBasket`, seg13:0465): entering, three times in four or never yet, plainly (`+0x3610` 0: its head let go, 0x1c4, then by temperament, 24 0x1c5, 27 0x2ce, 25 0x2d0, 26 0x2d1, else 0x2cf), else boldly (1: 0x1ca, 0x1c8), counting its peeks (`+0x3612`) where `PetParams` `+0xca`; each tick, a double-click and the apt's root holding fewer than ten (`+0x1e0`), it climbs out (0x4c); cued (6), it watches the mouse unless it is still and one time in two makes a sound (7); its script done, a draw of 2 to 7 over its peeks (unless its basket is 3 and the mouse not still), `+0xca` 0 or ten or more, it ends its peek (0x4b, its basket none); else, every basket been in, none, and its basket been in (unless 3 and the mouse not still), it ends its peek; else it climbs out (0x4c, its peeks none). Ending it (75; `DoCatEndPeekFromBasket`, seg13:090c): entering, boldly 0x1ca, then 0x1c4; its script done, in the baskets (0x49).
- [[measured]] With `TRACE_KIT=1` (`TRACE.TXT`'s 256) the driver gives the module command 5 at frame 10 (`CATZTRACE-KIT.OUT`); the trace keeps the basket's fields, `+0xca`, the root's count and the mouse's state. Over 800 frames, the cat in the baskets peeking and going back the while (`+0xca` 0), all 33 ticks held are as the reimplementation: beginning, in the baskets, peeking and ending its peek. Climbing out (76), jumping in (77) and dropping in (78) were not met so; they are since, in the Kit's longer traces (below), the fail-safe (79) too, every tick of them held as the reimplementation.
- [[read out]] Climbing out (76; `DoCatExitBasket`, seg13:09d3): entering, out (`+0x35fa` 1, its slot `0x3d8`, `0x9c` 1), 0x171; cued (0), it lets the basket go (`0xfc`) and has been in it (`+0x3614`); its script done, by temperament: 24 grooming (13); 27 after the cursor (`+0x35a4` 0x17), one time in two the mouse roused (kin 0x1b's `+0x25d0` 0, `+0x2622` and `+0x2674` 100), then one time in five scared (17), else playing (15); 25 the same, attacking (1); 26, its energy 50, one time in two eating (3), else affectionate (14); else one time in two affectionate, else playing with one of 6, 7 and 5 (a draw of 3, seg48:1b3a).
- [[measured]] With a double-click 20 frames of each 400 (`PetParams` `+0x46`), the cat climbs out as it peeks: over 1,200 frames, all 66 ticks held are as the reimplementation, climbing out among them (affectionate after); it did not go back.
- [[read out]] Back to its basket (`MakeCatReturnToBasket`, seg23:5838, the cat's slot `0x15c`, as the Kit's button has it): `0x9c` 1, its slot `0x168`, `+0x33be` 2, `+0x35fc` 1, and the Kit (global 22), whose entering takes 2 to jumping in (0x4d). Jumping in (77; `DoCatJumpInBasket`, seg13:0d29) and dropping in (78; `DoCatDropIntoBasket`, seg13:0e9a): entering, its basket's place its goal (its slot `0x3cc`), its words to it, and 0x1c4 jumping, 0x297 dropping (dropping lets the basket go first where `+0x226` of kin 0x18 is 0, and its fudgers); cued (0), in the basket; its script done, in the baskets (0x49, `+0x35f6` -1). Its fail-safe (global 23, `DoGSFailSafe`; 79, `DoCatFailSafeState`, seg13:1093): entering, `0x9c` 2 and 0x177; its script done, idling (6).
- [[measured]] Sent back to its basket at the 350th frame of each 400 where out of the Kit, over 1,200 frames the cat goes round it -- climbing out (playing or affectionate after), out in the pen, back, jumping in (77), in the baskets and peeking -- and all 77 ticks held are as the reimplementation. Dropping in (78) and the fail-safe were not met so; with the messages or the scruff too (`CATZTRACE-KIT.OUT`, `-KIT-MESSAGES-C4`, `-KIT-SCRUFF-MESSAGES-C4`) dropping in is held in each (3 to 5 ticks acted in) and the fail-safe in two, as the reimplementation.
- [[read out]] Dropping in is the user's: `DoDangling` (seg23), let go of with the cat within 20 across of its basket and 0 to 150 above it, in pose 0x5c, has `+0x33be` 4 and `+0x35fc` 1, its fudgers and motion stopped, the cat moved, and the Kit (22), whose entering takes 4 to dropping in (0x4e; by `+0x33be`, a jump table at seg13:015a: 1 beginning, 0x48; 2 walking to its basket's place, 150 above it, `+0x35ac` 1, 0x16; 3 jumping in, 0x4d; 4 dropping in). The fail-safe is the module's: `PetModule::DoDrawFrame` (seg34:3af3), an error kept in `XApex::theirError` (seg58:000a), clears it and puts the cat (kin 0x1d) in global state 23.
- [[measured]] So the driver takes the 350th frame of each 400, out of the Kit, in turn: the cat sent back to its basket; dropped in, as `DoDangling` leaves it (`+0x33be` 4, `+0x35fc` 1, the global machine's `0x1c` 22, unmoved); and the fail-safe (23), as after an error. Over 2,000 frames all 129 ticks held are as the reimplementation: among them both drops into the basket (78), entering (0x297) and in the baskets after, and the fail-safe (79) to idling, and after it fear (58, 59, 62) and swatting.

- [[measured]] CATZ.EXE runs on winbox.js to its playpen, and the cat can be read there a frame at a time: its `CatSprite` is found by its class's table (seg16:499a), and each frame drawn its states, action, frame, rectangle, fudgers and brains are kept. A kitten of the Adoption Kit starts in `initKit` (0x48), then sits in its basket (`inBaskets`, 0x49), peeks out of it and back (`peekFromBasket`, `endPeekFromBasket`, 0x4a and 0x4b) six times or so, then climbs out (`exitBasket`, 0x4c), walks (`locomote` and `performLocomote`, 22 and 23) and stalks (2). Walking, its rotation fudger turns it 5 a tick.

- [[read out]] What the Kit writes (corrections to the above first):
  - **`MakeCatReturnToBasket`:** sets `+0x33be` 2, and the Kit's entering then walks the cat to 150 above its basket (0x16, `+0x35a6` 0, `+0x35ac` 1), not jumping in. Arriving (`DoCatPerformLocomote`, seg25:1efa), `+0x33be` becomes 3 and the Kit is entered again, so it jumps in (0x4d).
  - **The Kit's entering:** always clears `+0x3612`. Leaving, `+0x35fa` is 0.
  - **Root `+0x1e0`:** not a count, but the basket the user holds down (11 to 15, else 0).
  - **In the moment, in the Kit** (`DoCatMoment`, seg25:36ab): `+0x33be` becomes 2, and its one choice is going back to its basket (0x16). That walk is without `+0x35fc`, so its reflexes stay on; not modelled.
  - **Beginning it (72):** entering, the cat is centred 100 above and left of the stage's corner (`+0x34cc`), 0x1c4 is pushed, the basket sprite is held to it (kin 0x18), and the baskets been in (`+0x3614`) are cleared. Its script done, it has no basket (`+0x35f6` -1) and is in the baskets.
  - **In the baskets (73), entering:** not on its way (`+0x35fc` 0). With no basket, it picks one (`PickANewBasket`, seg13:12bf, its slot `0x3d0`): `rand() % 5`, the next where it is the last it was in (`+0x35f8`), or the basket the user holds down, the draw spent all the same. It is then made again as that basket's kitten (`SetupNewBasket`, seg13:141d, its slot `0x3d4`), as below. Its count and wait (`+0x360e`, written nowhere else) are 0, so it peeks the tick after. With a basket already (a click on another), it is made again so, the basket lit, and it climbs out (0x4c).
  - **Made again (`SetupNewBasket`):** each basket is a breed. Baskets 0 to 4 are ASIAMESE.LNZ (Sophisto), ACLOWN.LNZ (Jester), APERSIAN.LNZ (Homebody), AHUNTER.LNZ (Pouncer) and ASCAREDY.LNZ (Scaredy). It loads that file, draws its brains again (`InitBrain`, one `rand()` per brain with a spread), faces 0 and lets its head go.
  - **Peeking (74), entering:** `rand() % 101` under 75, or no peeks yet, gives mode 0, else 1 (`+0x3610`). Peeks (`+0x3612`) go up one where `PetParams` `+0xca` is set. Its count is 0, and its tick runs at once.
  - **Peeking, each tick:** its count's first tick puts it back on its basket's place. A double-click and no basket held, it climbs out, `+0x3612` kept.
  - **Peeking, its script done:** going back, its basket is none (-1). Climbing out, `+0x3612` is 0, and the baskets been in are cleared first where all were.
  - **Climbing out (76):** entering, out (`+0x35fa` 1), its breed's description shown (`GiveBreedDescriptions`, its slot `0x3d8`). Cued (0), it lets the basket go and has been in it.
  - **Jumping in (77) and dropping in (78):** their scripts done, its basket is none, so the next is picked anew (not the same).
  - **`PetParams` `+0xca`:** never written by CATZDLL; its host sets it. At 0 the kittens only peek, never coming out of themselves, and no basket's text shows.
- [[read out]] The basket is one sprite, kin 0x18 (`art\basket.apt`, CATZDLL resource type 32513, 2000), 118 by 145.
  - **Where:** `GetBasketLocation` (seg13:113b, its slot `0x3cc`) puts it at its basket's rectangle from the Kit's origin (`PetParams` `+0xdc`, `+0xde`): basket 0 at (0xa5, 0xaa), 1 at (0xfd, 0xb9), 2 at (0x156, 0xbe), 3 at (0x1ae, 0xb4), 4 at (0x207, 0xaf). It answers the cat's place, `CATZ_BASKETS`.
  - **Its pictures:** 16500 and 16501 plain, 16510 and 16511 lit. Held to the cat, it is drawn over it.
  - **The highlight:** kin 0x19 (`baskhi.apt`, 3000), its pictures the lit ones, is shown over a basket held down (below), not one hovered over.
  - **Not found:** where the other four baskets are drawn is not in this code; the host's backdrop, it seems.
- [[read out]] A click on the cat's own basket while it is out sends it back. `BasketHitTest` lets go over basket n with `DoCommand(0x821 + n)`, which leaves message 0x16 + n, which `HandleCatAptMessages` gives to `MakeCatReturnToBasket`. A click on another basket gives the Kit's "put the kitten away" text. In 73 to 75, a click on its own basket has it climb out, and on another, it changes to that basket.
- [[read out]] Pressing the baskets (`XApt::BasketHitTest`, seg6:30ca), which the playpen's root runs each frame where none of its sprites took the mouse (seg6:18f8), in the Kit (`PetParams` `+0xc4`) with nothing held (the root's `+0x8a`):
  - **Down** (`PetParams` `+0x1e`): its press (`XApt::m_ptDown`) in basket n's box, the root's `+0x1e0` is 0xb + n, and the highlight is put over basket n's picture and shown, unless n is the cat's own basket (`+0x35f6`), which is lit instead (`SetCond` 2). Its test of `+0x1e0` first is `!x < 10`, always true.
  - **Up**, with a basket held: let go (`m_ptUp`) in the box of the basket held, none is held, and, neither climbing out nor on its way back (`IsBasketExitOrReturn`, seg13:16be, its slot `0x118`: `+0x35fc` or `+0x35fa`), its command (`DoCommand` 0x821 + n, message 0x16 + n); else message 0x2a ("Please wait for the kitten to return."). The highlight is hidden but in the Kit (global 22) neither. Let go elsewhere, none is held and it is hidden. While a basket is held it answers 1, taking the mouse.
  - **Its boxes,** from the Kit's origin, each edge within: the point less (0xa5, 0x64) tested against 7, 8 and 9 and constants, so 0 at x 0xae to 0x10b, y 0xb2 to 0x143; 1 at 0x111 to 0x163, 0xc1 to 0x152; 2 at 0x16a to 0x1bc, 0xc6 to 0x157; 3 at 0x1c2 to 0x214, 0xbc to 0x14d; 4 at 0x21b to 0x271, 0xb7 to 0x148 (`CATZ_BASKET_HITS`). The earlier read-out's lefts for 1 to 4, and 4's right, were wrong.
  - **What holds it:** peeking (74) with a basket held does not climb out but ends its peek, and `PickANewBasket` takes the held basket, hiding the highlight and lighting it (`SetCond` 2). The highlight is also hidden by `PickANewBasket` where shown, by `DoCatInBaskets` entering with a basket, and by the cat's own basket chosen in its baskets.
- [[inferred]] `CatzPet.basketHitTest` does so, keeping `+0x1e0` (the Kit's `rootCount`) and the highlight (`kitHighlight`), which entering 73 or 76 hides. A unit test presses another basket while the kitten peeks: held and highlighted; let go elsewhere, nothing; let go in it, the kitten changes to it and climbs out of it; its own pressed climbing out, lit, and let go, message 0x2a.
- [[measured]] `catzKit` reports what it writes (`kit`, and `remade` for the basket whose kitten it becomes), and picks its basket entering the baskets. Being ticked mid-script (`done` false), peeking and climbing out stop where their scripts are not done. The six Kit traces still agree tick for tick. Their bookkeeping holds only beginning's clearing of the baskets been in, all five agreeing: the Kit's other writes come as it changes state, which the bookkeeping does not hold, so they rest on the read-out.

- [[inferred]] The Kit live (`CatzPet` with `kit`: its origin and `PetParams` `+0xca`). It keeps the Kit's fields (`kit`), `+0x33be` and `+0x35fc`, and begins in the Kit (global 22) as `SetupCatForAdoption` leaves it: basket 0, `+0x33be` 1, not idling first.
  - **The begin:** `catzGlobalBegin` begins 22 by `+0x33be`, given its basket's place (`basketAt`: `CATZ_BASKETS` from the origin).
  - **Entering and leaving:** entering clears its peeks; leaving, however left, clears `+0x35fa`. Entering the baskets clears `+0x35fc`.
  - **Its states:** they run as they are entered (72 to 75 among them). Peeking and climbing out also run mid-script with their scripts not done.
  - **Made again:** put in a basket (`remade`), the kitten is made again as that basket's breed by whoever runs it, its brains and centres given back.
  - **Messages:** read through `catzKitMessage` with the shelf, `+0x35fa`, its last basket and its stage, and none while on its way (`+0x35fc`). Its own basket clicked while out, it is sent back (`MakeCatReturnToBasket`: `+0x33be` 2, `+0x35fc` 1, the Kit). In the baskets, another basket clicked becomes its basket.
  - **Headless** (`test/behaviour/catz_pet_test.ts`), three days of 3,000 ticks, a double-click 20 of each 400 and its own basket clicked now and then: it began, sat in its baskets, peeked, came out, was sent back, walked to above its basket and jumped in (77), made again as each basket it was put in (never the same twice running), without fault.

- [[inferred]] The Kit in the viewer (an Adoption Kit box, then Live).
  - **The baskets:** its origin is (0, 40). The five baskets are drawn at their rectangles from it, each the basket's back and handle (16500) behind the kitten and its woven front (16501) over it, so it sits in it. The kitten's own basket is drawn lit (16510, 16511) while it is out or climbing out, jumping or dropping in (76 to 78), as `SetCond` 2 and 1 light and darken it.
  - **Its words:** climbing out (76), its breed's description (`GiveBreedDescriptions`, strings 0x233d + its basket) is shown above the stage, and cleared back in the baskets (73).
  - **The kitten:** begun 100 above and left of the stage's corner.
  - **Made again:** put in a basket, it is made again as that basket's breed: its file averaged with its kitten's by the slider, its brains made as the Kit makes them (`initCatzBrains`, `kit`). The body is made again too (`CatzBody.rebreed`) and seated on the basket's place.
  - **Clicks:** the left button's press and release on the baskets go to `basketHitTest` after each tick, where neither the cat nor a thing took the press; the basket held is drawn lit, as the highlight. A double-click (`dblclick`) is the pet's `doubleClicked` for one tick. Basket 1 pressed while it peeked from basket 4: it ended its peek, was put in basket 1 and climbed out as a Jester.
  - **The mouse:** hidden in its hole, it is told to the cat as in it (state 1), whatever its machine has yet. Before, its first tick's state 0 had the cat see it out and play at once.
  - **Run so:** the kitten began, peeked from its baskets and went back, climbed out (76) and played or was affectionate. Its own basket clicked once it was out, it walked back to above it, jumped in (77) and was in the baskets again. The screenshot had a yellow kitten in basket 4 and, jumping in, coming down into it. Climbing out of basket 2 it showed "The proud Sophisto is an elegant breed."
